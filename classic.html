// about

A solo project
from Istanbul.

KAOSMOS is the solo ambient, drone and experimental project from Istanbul, started in 2013. She creates a fusion of mysterious atmospheric themes and humming cold layers of synthesizers, bringing KAOSMOS to life.

With her music she aims to emancipate her listeners from the reality we live in on this planet — for the length of a record, at least.
